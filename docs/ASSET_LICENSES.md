# Assets and credits

| File or source | Origin and redistribution |
|---|---|
| `assets/people/shopkeeper.glb` | [Man by Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/HMnuH5geEG), Public Domain [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 skinned mesh and original skeletal clips, with revised smooth normals and skin, hair and cloth roughness. |
| `assets/people/neighbor.glb` | [Woman Casual by Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/jpKRgGDxhk), Public Domain CC0 1.0. CC0 skinned mesh and original skeletal clips, with revised smooth normals and skin, hair and cloth roughness. |
| Harbor District | Original tileable limewash and timber textures generated by `ci/surfaces.py`, geometry, color palette, storefront lettering, products and props constructed by `world/district.gd`. |
| Sound and branding | Original Harbor Lights menu composition rendered by `ci/menu_music.py`, synthesized audio in `core/sound.gd`, original vector mark in `assets/ui/mark.svg`. |
| Typeface | Godot's bundled fallback and the device's installed serif face. |
| Engine | [Godot Engine and third-party license notices](https://godotengine.org/license/). Accessible through Credits. |

Both character files were obtained from the creators' Poly Pizza listings. The scene scales them and plays their idle, walking and running clips. The project does not redistribute a commercial asset pack.
