# PC simulator references for Qofeno on Android

## Can the uploaded Windows ZIP become a standalone APK?

The archive contains the shipped Windows version of *Supermarket Simulator*. I read the executable headers inside the 1,294,230,439-byte Git LFS object. The game executable, `GameAssembly.dll`, and `UnityPlayer.dll` all identify as Windows PE machine type `0x8664` (AMD64). The package includes Windows builds of Steam and Microsoft gaming services. It contains cooked Unity scenes and IL2CPP metadata. There are no original C# files or Unity Editor project settings. [Full archive and native-code audit](COUNTER_SERVICE.md)

For comparison, I inspected the released Qofeno 0.5.1 APK. Its `lib/arm64-v8a/libgodot_android.so` identifies as ELF machine 183 (AArch64). It also carries Android ARMv7 and x86-64 libraries. Even on an x86-64 Android device, a Windows PE file expects a different loader and platform services. Changing the extension or placing those DLLs in an APK cannot start the game. [Android native ABIs](https://developer.android.com/ndk/guides/abis)

With the developer's buildable Unity project, a port would compile an Android player and gameplay code, replace or disable desktop-only integrations, adapt controls, and profile lighting, memory and rendering on phones. Starting from the shipped archive instead requires reconstructing or rewriting systems. Decompilation can explain a till or a steering routine, but it cannot reproduce the original project by itself. For playing the PC edition on a phone today, Valve's Steam Link runs the game on the player's PC and streams the picture and input. [Unity Android build process](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/android-BuildProcess.html) · [Valve's Android setup](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/7112-CD02-7B57-59F8)

## What the games show

I reviewed 35 published gallery frames, five per game. These are screen observations. Still frames can establish where a gauge sits or how a shop reads after dark. They cannot establish traffic logic, shader cost or frame rate.

| PC game | Visible detail | A Qofeno phone design to try |
|---|---|---|
| [Supermarket Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2670630/) | At the till, a customer, belt and register share the frame. At the ordering monitor, product rows fill the screen. | Keep the queue visible while scanning. Give shopping the whole monitor and enlarge each order row for touch. |
| [Motel Manager Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2594540/) | The roadside sign identifies the site from a distance after dark. At the pump, the hose and truck show which vehicle is being served. | Light the harbor sign and loading bay as landmarks. Bind the unloading prompt to the vehicle beside the bay. |
| [Internet Cafe Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1136160/) | Seated customers have feedback above them. Focusing on the owner's monitor reveals an ordering window with small desktop controls. | Show a waiting customer's concern at their seat. Give the monitor an explicit touch exit that returns the player to the same desk. |
| [Internet Cafe Simulator 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1563180/) | The café's glowing sign is visible from the alley. A carried monitor takes up enough of the view to communicate its size. | Give a delivery carton a visible silhouette and keep the route to the doorway visible around it. |
| [Gas Station Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1149620/) | At sunset, the pump canopy silhouettes the forecourt. Inside, shelving and overhead lights direct the eye down the aisle. | Walk the quay at dusk and check that a first-time player can find the store entrance and till before adding more lamps. |
| [TCG Card Shop Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3070070/) | Players sit opposite each other at a table. A pack reveal brings the cards forward and puts their total underneath. | Inspect a single product at a readable size, then return to the shelf and viewing angle where it was selected. |
| [Car For Sale Simulator 2023](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2248760/) | The driving view shows the road beside a speedometer. A negotiation view faces the seller and shows the vehicle's condition. | Make each driving control reachable while the next turn stays visible. Show cargo details when the estate is parked. |

Of the commercial references, the uploaded *Supermarket Simulator* build supports the deeper native-code findings in the [checkout study](COUNTER_SERVICE.md). For *Motel Manager Simulator* the public depot lists an Unreal Windows package. For *Internet Cafe Simulator 2* it lists a Unity managed gameplay DLL. I located no developer-published gameplay source for either commercial title. A depot filename establishes packaging and says nothing about how a customer pays. [Motel depot](https://steamdb.info/depot/2594541/) · [Café depot](https://steamdb.info/depot/1563181/)
